Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Moon Gets One Star"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-moon-gets-one-star-768236e5): the funniest one-star review of the Moon in 120 characters or fewer.

## The entry

> One star. It follows me home every night, stares through my window, and never once blinks. Reported.

(100 characters, one line, one star review of the Moon itself.)

## How it works

`main.py` writes 10 fresh candidate reviews, scores each against the judge's
brief in the brief's own order — a review of the Moon itself, funnier on
first read, reads like a real one-star review, a specific surprising
grievance (no craters, cheese, or brightness jokes), every word earning its
place — picks the highest scorer, validates the 1–120 character gate, and
writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
